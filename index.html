The profile

A sales leader who learned to grow markets on the ground — and now teaches the data and AI skills the next generation will grow them with.

Pranav Dinesh Pyati is the Executive Director of Inera SOI — the School of Intelligence — the AI education and skilling vertical of INERA GROUP (Inera Software Pvt. Ltd.). He leads SOI's planning, college partnerships and growth with his own team.

He brings 11 years of industry experience as a corporate trainer, business development and sales professional, working across mobility, fintech, EV and customer-centric industries — building teams, opening markets and delivering measurable results.

Alongside his corporate career, he trains and mentors students and aspiring professionals in Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Data Science and AI/ML — pairing classroom concepts with the business problems he has solved in the field. A placement expert, he guides learners into their first roles, and as a professional social media and LinkedIn expert he helps them build the personal brand that gets them noticed.

His record includes exceeding 120% of sales targets for six consecutive months at Paytm, and leading large-scale operations as Branch Head at Udday Softech, onboarding more than 6,000 cabs and 3,000 auto-rickshaws across multiple cities.
